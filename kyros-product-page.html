Brewing Recommendations

This coffee shines in espresso and milk-based drinks. The natural process creates enough body and sweetness to cut through milk beautifully. For espresso: 18-20g dose, 1:2-1:2.5 ratio, 25-30 second extraction. For pour-over: 15:1 water ratio, 200°F, medium-fine grind. For cold brew: coarse grind, 1:8 ratio, 16-24 hours.
